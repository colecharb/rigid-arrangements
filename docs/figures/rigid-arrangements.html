Rigid arrangements of unit circles

Every rigid arrangement of n unit circles, up to rotation, reflection and relabeling. Rigid means locally rigid in the sense of Asimow–Roth. Hold every tangency fixed as a bar of length 2, and no small motion changes the shape. Each count below marked proved is a theorem in Lean 4, covering every arrangement in the plane, not only those on the triangular lattice.

Off the lattice

Every arrangement above sits on the triangular lattice, and for up to eight circles that is a theorem. From eleven circles on it is no longer true. Each group of triangles linked through shared edges lies on its own copy of the lattice, and an arrangement is off the lattice exactly when its contacts point in more than one direction modulo 60°. Below, contacts and triangles are colored by that direction class, shared circles (hinges) have a heavy outline, and circles in no triangle are dashed. Details are in docs/offlattice.md.

The drawings are generated from lattice coordinates: a site (a, b) has center a·(2, 0) + b·(1, √3), so two circles touch exactly when their sites differ by one of the six unit steps. These are the coordinates used by the Lean definitions triangleSites, rhombusSites, and so on, in RigidArrangements/Small.lean, and by the certified catalogues latticeCatalogue7 and latticeCatalogue8 in RigidArrangements/LatticeCert.lean.
